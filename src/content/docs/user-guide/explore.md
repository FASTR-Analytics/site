The **Explore** page lets you look through a results package without building anything. It shows each module's results as ready-made tables, charts, and maps, so you can check numbers, spot problems, and get a feel for the data before you put a visualization in a deck or report.

## Choosing what to look at
<!-- help#explore-choose -->

The bar at the top of the page sets what you are exploring. Choose a **results package** - the pinned package is selected for you - and a **scope**. Then pick a tab for the type of data: **HMIS**, **HFA**, or **ICEH**. A tab only appears if the package contains that data.

Below the bar, choose a **module** and, where the module offers several, a **view**. Views are organized around common questions - data quality views such as **Outliers**, **Completeness**, and **Internal consistency**, coverage views such as **Coverage over time** and **Coverage by area**, service views such as **Service disruption**, and HFA views such as **Indicators by survey round**. Many views can be shown as a **Table**, **Over time**, as a **Chart**, or on a **Map** - use the type toggle to switch.

FASTR remembers the data type, module, view, and display type you chose the next time you open the page.

## Narrowing the results

The toolbar above the results lets you change the geographic level (**National** or a lower admin area level), pick particular areas or indicators, choose a time period such as **Last 12 months** or **Last year**, and set the time grain to **Month**, **Quarter**, or **Year**.

In table view, click a column header to sort, use **Find column** to jump to a column, and click **Download** to save the table as a CSV file.

## Explore and your scope

Explore shows a package through one scope at a time, so what you see depends on the scope you choose. If you are limited to particular scopes, the scope list only offers yours and the page opens on the first of them.

Explore is read-only. To keep a chart, create a visualization on the Products page or insert one into a deck or report - see [Visualizations](/user-guide/visualizations/).

## Common issues

**"No results package is ready yet"**: Your administrator hasn't generated a package. Ask them to generate one on the Results page.

**"No data for this selection"**: The current filters or scope leave nothing to show. Widen the period, choose a higher geographic level, or switch scope.

**A module or tab is missing**: The package doesn't include that data type or module, or your scope leaves it out.
