FASTR sends you an email when you, or another person, sign in to your account from a device (for example a computer or a phone) that FASTR does not recognise. The email has one button, and pressing it signs that device out of FASTR.

## If you pressed the button

The device described in the email is now signed out of FASTR. The button does this immediately, and you are not asked whether you are sure.

If you do not know whether the sign-in was yours, read [How to tell whether a sign-in was yours](#how-to-tell-whether-a-sign-in-was-yours). If you are still not sure, follow the steps for a sign-in that was not yours.

### The sign-in was yours

The email does not mean that anything is wrong with your account. You signed your own device out of FASTR, so you need to sign in again. You do not need to change your password.

1. Go to the FASTR web address that you normally use. Each country has its own address, so this page does not show it.
2. Sign in with your email address and password.

You may receive the same email again when you sign in. If you receive it, do not press the button.

### The sign-in was not yours

Someone else signed in to your account, and pressing the button was the right thing to do. The device that this person used is now signed out of FASTR. Change your password now, so that this person cannot sign in again.

1. Go to the FASTR web address that you normally use, and sign in.
2. Click the person icon at the top right of the page, then click **Your profile**.
3. Click **Manage account**, then **Security**.
4. Click **Update password**. If the form asks for your current password, enter it.
5. Enter your new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**.
6. Make sure the **Sign out of all other devices** checkbox is selected, then click **Save**. The checkbox is already selected when the form opens. It signs out every other device that is signed in to your account.

Then tell your FASTR administrator that someone else signed in to your account. Your FASTR administrator is the person who manages FASTR user accounts in your country. If you cannot sign in, contact your FASTR administrator.

## How to tell whether a sign-in was yours

The email shows the type of device, the operating system (for example Windows or Android), the IP address (a number that identifies the internet connection), the location, and the sign-in method. The sign-in was yours if you signed in to FASTR yourself shortly before the email arrived, and if the type of device and the operating system match the device you used.

## If you receive the email again

FASTR sends the email each time it does not recognise the device that you sign in from, so you may receive it again. Before you press the button, decide whether the sign-in was yours.

If the sign-in was yours, you do not need to do anything. Do not press the button, because it is not a way to confirm that the sign-in was yours. If the sign-in was not yours, or you are not sure, press the button, then [change your password](#the-sign-in-was-not-yours) and tell your FASTR administrator.
